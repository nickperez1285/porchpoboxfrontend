## Reminders

- **Link targets:** always point to `/porch-piracy-report`, `/stop-porch-pirates`, or `/porch-safety-score` (never straight to `/plans` unless asked).
- **Never pay for links** or accept reciprocal exchanges outside your genuine partner network.
- **HARO cadence:** reply daily; consistency beats cleverness.
- **Track every pitch** in a simple sheet: source, date, contact, outcome.
- **Reuse stats consistently** across pitches — keep the annual report as your single source of truth.
